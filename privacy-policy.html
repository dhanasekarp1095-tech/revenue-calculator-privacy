Privacy Policy

Revenue Calculator

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Revenue Calculator ("the App") is a personal finance and monthly money planning application designed to help users manage income, expenses, loans, savings, financial transactions, reminders, and reports.

1. Information You Enter

The App may store information that you voluntarily enter, including monthly income, expenses, loan and EMI details, fixed savings, flexible savings, monthly financial planning information, and financial transaction information imported from bank statements. This information is used to provide the App's features.

2. Bank Statement Import

The App provides an optional bank statement PDF import feature. When you choose to import a bank statement, the App processes the selected PDF on your device to identify transaction information and may automatically categorize transactions.

  • The PDF is selected by you.
  • Bank statement information is processed locally on your device.
  • Imported transaction information is stored locally on your device.
  • The App does not intentionally upload your bank statement or its financial contents to a remote server.
  • We do not sell or rent your bank statement information.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • Calculate monthly income and expenses
  • Track loans and EMIs
  • Track savings
  • Categorize imported transactions
  • Generate monthly financial summaries and reports
  • Provide reminders that you configure
  • Display your financial information within the App

4. Data Storage

The App stores financial information locally on your device using local application storage. The App does not require an online account to use its core features.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your financial information with advertisers or other third parties. The current version of the App does not use advertising services to collect or profile your financial information.

6. Internet and Network Access

The core financial planning, transaction storage, and bank statement processing features are designed to operate locally on your device. The App does not intentionally transmit your financial information to a remote server for storage.

7. Notifications

The App may send local notifications for reminders that you configure. These reminders are generated by the App and do not require your financial information to be sent to an external notification service.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your financial information remains on your device until you delete it using the App's available functionality or remove the App and its associated local data from your device. The App does not currently provide an online user account, so there is no server-side account containing your financial information that can be deleted.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by the App. Financial information is intended to remain on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

10. Children's Privacy

Revenue Calculator is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Third-Party Services

The current version of Revenue Calculator does not intentionally share users' financial information with third-party services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available through the Privacy Policy URL associated with the App.
